About Joveo:

Every company says they're "AI-first." We actually are. Joveo's recruitment

advertising platform processes millions of hiring decisions through machine

learning, real-time bidding, and predictive analytics — helping the world's

largest employers find the right people, faster and fairer. But we're not

done. Not even close.

Role: Graduate Developer

Location: Remote

Role Overview:

We are hiring a Graduate Developer to join Joveo's engineering team

straight out of university. You will work across backend, frontend,

or full stack development depending on your strengths and interests —

shipping real features, learning fast, and growing under the mentorship

of experienced engineers.

Key Responsibilities:

- Write production code under the guidance of senior developers

- Learn modern development practices including testing, CI/CD, and code review

- Contribute to feature delivery, bug fixing, and ongoing platform improvements

- Participate in team rituals — standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives

- Build strong technical foundations in at least one specialization over time

- Develop professional skills through structured mentorship and career development

Required Skills & Qualifications:

- Recent graduate with a Bachelor's or Master's in a technology-related field

- Strong programming fundamentals in at least one language

- Familiarity with basic web development concepts — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, APIs

- Exposure to version control (Git) and collaborative development workflows

- Desire to work across both frontend and backend over time

- Excellent communication skills and a strong bias toward learning by doing
