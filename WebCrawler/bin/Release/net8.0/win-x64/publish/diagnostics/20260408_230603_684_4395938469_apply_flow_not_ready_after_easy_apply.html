Company Description
Belmont73 AI combines capital and artificial intelligence to drive the success of its portfolio companies and collaborates with venture capital partners through strategic AI operating agreements. The company was founded by experienced professionals with backgrounds in big-tech, Wall Street, and consulting, offering a comprehensive approach to fostering innovation. Belmont73 AI is dedicated to leveraging cutting-edge technologies to provide transformative solutions to its partners and clients.

Role Description
This is a full-time remote role for a Senior Full Stack Software Engineer specializing in Node.js, NestJS, and React. The individual will design, develop, and maintain scalable, high-performance web applications and services. Responsibilities include collaborating with cross-functional teams, writing clean and efficient code for both client-side and server-side components, optimizing system performance, and participating in code reviews. This role also involves contributing to architecture decisions and mentoring junior developers.

Qualifications

  • Back-End Web Development skills, including experience with Node.js and NestJS
  • Front-End Development experience, particularly with React and expertise in CSS
  • Strong foundation in Full-Stack and Software Development methodologies
  • Proficiency in problem-solving, debugging, and writing clean, maintainable code
  • Familiarity with cloud services, DevOps practices, or CI/CD pipelines is a plus
  • Excellent collaboration and communication skills
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field is preferred
